# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

## 我们的承诺 / Our Pledge

为了营造一个开放和欢迎的环境，我们作为贡献者和维护者承诺：无论年龄、体型、残障、族裔、性别认同与表达、经验水平、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教或性取向如何，参与本项目的每个人都免受骚扰。
In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## 我们的标准 / Our Standards

**鼓励的行为 / Positive behavior includes:**

- 使用友好和包容的语言 / Using welcoming and inclusive language
- 尊重不同的观点和经验 / Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- 优雅地接受建设性批评 / Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- 对社区成员保持善意 / Showing empathy towards other community members

**不可接受的行为 / Unacceptable behavior includes:**

- 性化的言语或暗示、人身攻击 / Sexualized language, personal attacks
- 骚扰性评论、发布他人隐私信息 / Trolling, insulting comments, publishing others' private information
- 其他在专业环境中不合适的行为 / Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## 责任 / Responsibilities

项目维护者有权删除、编辑与本行为准则不符的评论、提交、代码、Issue 及其他贡献内容，并对违反本准则的参与者采取封禁等管理措施。
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for other behaviors deemed inappropriate.

## 范围 / Scope

本准则适用于项目空间（GitHub Issues、Pull Requests、Discussions）以及代表本项目的公开场合。
This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and in public spaces when representing the project.

## 执行 / Enforcement

如遇滥用行为，请通过 [GitHub 私信联系维护者](https://github.com/ohto-ai) 或在 [Security 渠道](SECURITY.md) 报告。维护者会尽快审阅并作出回应。维护者有义务对报告人信息保密。
Instances of abusive behavior may be reported by contacting [the maintainer on GitHub](https://github.com/ohto-ai). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's identity will be kept confidential.
